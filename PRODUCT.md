# PRODUCT: beneru.spawntel.com

**What it is:** the personal-brand site of Reuben Magallanes (Beneru), Marketing Head and Co-Founder of Spawntel. Niche: AI Direction & Marketing Systems.

**Who visits:** small-business owners and marketing leads who saw Reuben's content or DMs, mostly on a phone, deciding whether he can take manual marketing work (content, inbound DMs, lead follow-up) off their hands.

**The one job of the page:** make a visitor believe the systems are real, then book a free 30-minute call on Spawntel (every booking CTA carries `shared/funnel.ts` attribution).

**Proof rules:** every number must trace to `src/data/work.ts`. No testimonials, no invented metrics, no "trusted by" rows. Meta DM status stays honest (dry run, pending Meta App Review). Say "Co-Founder of Spawntel", never "Partner".

**Brand:** official Spawntel palette only (D-053): Violet Brand `#6700FF` family, Bright `#8F4DFF`, Soft `#B79BFF`, neutrals `#0D0814` / `#15111E` / `#F0EDF7` / `#FFFFFF`. Type: Poppins (headings) + General Sans (body), no monospace. Copy voice: plain, direct, no em or en dashes.

**Design direction (2026-11-21):** light Ghost White story page in the structure of wishlabs.ai (original assets only): wordmark hero over a layered parallax cloud landscape, calm light sections, immersive cloud bands. Owner feedback: must feel clean and uncluttered, never busy.
